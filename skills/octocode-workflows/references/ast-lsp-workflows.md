# AST and LSP workflows

These are evidence techniques, not execution modes. The orchestrator assigns an evidence actor and keeps authority and ledger ownership. `octocode-research` owns tool choice, live schemas, continuation handling, and semantic limits; `octocode-architect` owns architecture interpretation, review angles, and change discipline.

## AST workflow

1. Frame roots, revision, invariant, and preserved outcome. Use Research's layout/text tools to locate the surface, inventory declarations, consume required pages, and record unparsed or unsupported scope.
2. Inspect actual syntax at observed lines before matching. AST is good for declaration, branch, loop, error, allocation, and repeated-shape candidates; it cannot prove symbol identity, a live call, equivalent behavior, or a defect. Account for modifiers, return types, and structural variants rather than treating one shape as universal.
3. Exact-read decisive matches and a healthy contrasting case. The healthy case identifies an invariant to preserve, not a template to copy. Treat AST/graph edges as candidates, then resolve identity and consumers with LSP.
4. Trace relevant producer, transform, boundary, and consumer, including generated registrations and dynamic dispatch that syntax may miss. Use runtime or measured evidence for behavioral, performance, and lifetime claims; refresh changed structural evidence after an authorized edit.

## LSP workflow

1. Anchor exact file, identifier, and line; resolve the definition and inspect alternatives. Disambiguate shadowing, overloads, aliases, interfaces, and same-name lookalikes before following callers or references.
2. Establish project root, parsing support, index freshness, fallback, and diagnostic coverage before trusting references, callers, implementations, or types. Unsupported parsing, stale indexing, generated code, plugins, callbacks, and dynamic registrations are coverage limits, not absence.
3. Trace semantic edges in both directions and exact-read decisive transforms, exports, registrations, generated consumers, and dynamic boundaries LSP cannot prove. A call hierarchy omits non-call uses, stored callbacks, aliases, and registry entries.
4. Re-resolve after a change; apply Architect's [proof bar for deletion/unification](../../octocode-architect/references/change-discipline.md#delete-junk-and-unify).

An empty AST/LSP result, missing graph edge, or tool agreement is never proof of absence or runtime behavior. Record source/index identity and partial coverage in the [ledger](verification.md#keep-one-compact-coverage-ledger); use the identity guard for runtime rows.
